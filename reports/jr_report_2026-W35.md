- **+12 points** for each "core" stack item (Linux, Python, Bash, AWS/Azure/GCP, Docker, K8s, CI/CD, Git, Terraform/IaC)
- **+4 points** for each "supporting" item (Networking, Monitoring, DBs, Virtualization, AD)
